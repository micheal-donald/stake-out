# Responsible Gambling Policy

**Last Updated:** November 21, 2025
**Version:** 1.0

## Our Commitment

Battle Arena is committed to promoting responsible gambling and protecting players from the harmful effects of problem gambling. We believe that gambling should be an enjoyable form of entertainment, not a way to make money or escape problems.

## 1. Warning Signs of Problem Gambling

You may have a gambling problem if you:
- Spend more money gambling than you can afford
- Need to gamble with increasing amounts of money
- Feel restless or irritable when trying to cut down
- Gamble to escape problems or relieve feelings of helplessness, guilt, or depression
- Lie to family members or others to hide gambling
- Jeopardize or lose important relationships, jobs, or opportunities because of gambling
- Rely on others to provide money to relieve desperate financial situations caused by gambling

## 2. Responsible Gambling Tools

### 2.1 Deposit Limits
Set limits on how much you can deposit:
- **Daily limit:** Maximum amount you can deposit in 24 hours
- **Weekly limit:** Maximum amount you can deposit in 7 days
- **Monthly limit:** Maximum amount you can deposit in 30 days

**How to set limits:**
1. Go to Account Settings
2. Select "Responsible Gambling"
3. Set your preferred limits
4. Limits take effect immediately
5. Increases have a 24-hour cooling-off period

### 2.2 Self-Exclusion
Temporarily or permanently block your account:

**Duration Options:**
- 24 hours
- 7 days
- 30 days
- 6 months
- Permanent

**During self-exclusion:**
- You cannot log in
- You cannot place bets
- You cannot deposit funds
- You can withdraw remaining balance

**How to self-exclude:**
1. Go to Account Settings
2. Select "Responsible Gambling"
3. Choose "Self-Exclusion"
4. Select duration
5. Confirm with your password

**Reactivation:**
- Short-term exclusions (24h-30d) lift automatically
- Long-term exclusions (6m+) require contacting support

### 2.3 Reality Checks
Reminder pop-ups appear every 60 minutes of continuous play showing:
- How long you've been playing
- Your net win/loss for the session
- Option to take a break or continue

### 2.4 Session Limits
Set maximum playing time:
- Choose session duration (30 min, 1 hour, 2 hours, etc.)
- Automatic logout when limit reached
- Must log in again to resume playing

### 2.5 Loss Limits
Set maximum loss amounts:
- Daily loss limit
- Weekly loss limit
- Monthly loss limit

### 2.6 Account Activity History
View complete history of:
- All bets placed
- Deposits and withdrawals
- Win/loss statistics
- Time spent playing

## 3. Gambling Responsibly - Best Practices

### 3.1 Set a Budget
- Only gamble with money you can afford to lose
- Never gamble with borrowed money
- Set a budget before you start
- Stop when you reach your budget limit

### 3.2 Set Time Limits
- Decide how long you'll play before starting
- Take regular breaks
- Don't gamble when tired or stressed

### 3.3 Keep It Fun
- Gambling should be entertainment, not income
- Don't chase losses
- Accept that you'll probably lose in the long run
- Never gamble to solve financial problems

### 3.4 Balance Your Life
- Don't let gambling interfere with:
  - Work or studies
  - Family and relationships
  - Other hobbies and interests
  - Financial responsibilities

### 3.5 Know the Odds
- The house always has an edge
- You cannot predict or control outcomes
- "Hot streaks" are random chance, not skill

## 4. Underage Gambling Prevention

### 4.1 Age Verification
- All users must be 18+ years old
- Date of birth verified at registration
- Accounts automatically suspended if underage

### 4.2 Protecting Minors
- Never share your account with minors
- Keep your password confidential
- Log out when finished playing
- Use parental controls on shared devices

## 6. Account Monitoring

We monitor accounts for signs of problem gambling:
- Unusual betting patterns
- Excessive time spent playing
- Large losses in short periods
- Repeated failed deposit attempts

If we detect concerning patterns, we may:
- Send a concerned message
- Suggest setting limits
- Recommend self-exclusion
- Restrict account temporarily

## 7. Family and Friends

### 7.1 If You're Concerned About Someone
- Talk to them in a calm, non-judgmental way
- Encourage them to seek help
- Contact support organizations
- Consider using self-exclusion tools together

### 7.2 Financial Protection
- Never lend money to someone with a gambling problem
- Help them set up deposit limits
- Encourage professional counseling

## 8. Gambling Therapy

Professional help is available:
- **Individual counseling**
- **Group therapy**
- **Financial counseling**
- **Family therapy**

Contact us at support@battlearena.com for referrals to licensed therapists specializing in gambling addiction.
