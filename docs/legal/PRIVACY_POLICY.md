# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** November 21, 2025
**Version:** 1.0
**Effective Date:** November 21, 2025

## 1. Introduction

Battle Arena ("we," "us," or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our Service.

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Personal Information
When you register, we collect:
- **Name/Username**
- **Email address**
- **Date of birth** (for age verification)
- **Phone number** (for M-Pesa transactions)
- **Password** (stored as encrypted hash)

### 2.2 Financial Information
- M-Pesa transaction IDs
- Deposit and withdrawal history
- Account balance
- Betting history

### 2.3 Automatically Collected Information
- **IP address**
- **Browser type and version**
- **Device information**
- **Login timestamps**
- **Game play data** (bets, wins, losses)
- **Cookies and similar tracking technologies**

### 2.4 Communications
- Support tickets
- Emails you send us
- Chat messages (if applicable)

## 3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

### 3.1 Provide the Service
- Create and manage your account
- Process deposits and withdrawals
- Enable gameplay
- Send transaction confirmations

### 3.2 Security and Fraud Prevention
- Verify your identity (KYC)
- Detect and prevent fraud
- Enforce our Terms of Service
- Monitor for suspicious activity

### 3.3 Communications
- Send verification emails
- Notify you of account changes
- Send password reset links
- Provide customer support
- Send important Service updates

### 3.4 Legal Compliance
- Comply with legal obligations
- Respond to legal requests
- Protect our legal rights
- Prevent illegal activities

### 3.5 Service Improvement
- Analyze usage patterns
- Improve user experience
- Develop new features
- Fix bugs and issues

## 4. How We Share Your Information

### 4.1 We DO NOT Sell Your Information
We will never sell your personal information to third parties.

### 4.2 Service Providers
We may share your information with:
- **Payment processors** (M-Pesa/Safaricom) - for transactions
- **Email service providers** (SendGrid) - for sending emails
- **Hosting providers** - for server infrastructure
- **Analytics services** - for usage analytics

### 4.3 Legal Requirements
We may disclose your information if required by:
- Law enforcement requests
- Court orders
- Legal proceedings
- Government regulations

### 4.4 Business Transfers
If we are involved in a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, your information may be transferred as part of that transaction.

## 5. Data Security

### 5.1 Security Measures
We implement industry-standard security measures including:
- **Encryption in transit** (SSL/TLS)
- **Encrypted password storage** (bcrypt)
- **Secure database access**
- **Regular security audits**
- **Access controls and authentication**

### 5.2 Account Security
You are responsible for:
- Keeping your password confidential
- Not sharing your account
- Notifying us of unauthorized access

### 5.3 No Guarantee
While we strive to protect your information, no method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security.

## 6. Data Retention

### 6.1 Active Accounts
We retain your information for as long as your account is active.

### 6.2 Closed Accounts
After account closure, we retain:
- Transaction records: 5 years (legal requirement)
- KYC documents: 5 years (anti-money laundering)
- Other data: 30 days (then deleted)

### 6.3 Legal Obligations
We may retain data longer if required by law or ongoing legal proceedings.

## 7. Your Rights

### 7.1 Access
You can access your personal information by logging into your account.

### 7.2 Correction
You can update your information in account settings.

### 7.3 Deletion
You can request account deletion by contacting support@battlearena.com. Note: Some data must be retained for legal compliance.

### 7.4 Data Portability
You can request a copy of your data in machine-readable format.

### 7.5 Opt-Out
You can opt out of:
- Marketing emails (we don't send marketing emails by default)
- Non-essential cookies

You CANNOT opt out of:
- Service-related emails (verification, password reset, etc.)
- Essential cookies required for the Service to function

## 8. Cookies and Tracking

### 8.1 What Are Cookies
Cookies are small text files stored on your device.

### 8.2 Types of Cookies We Use

**Essential Cookies:**
- Authentication tokens
- Session management
- CSRF protection

**Analytics Cookies:**
- Usage statistics
- Performance monitoring
- Error tracking (Sentry)

### 8.3 Managing Cookies
You can disable cookies in your browser settings. However, this may affect Service functionality.

## 9. Children's Privacy
